Thinking Pants Categories

Boost shows a category about twice as often; Off keeps its questions but never puts it on a board. Takes effect from the next board. These are the defaults for every venue; a code's Categories button can change them for that venue only.

The category dial turns on once you run thinking_pants_category_settings.sql in Neon. Until then every category is Normal.

Force Reset a Room

Bounces that room's TV back to its lobby-code screen and frees the code. Use this if a room is stuck or abandoned.
