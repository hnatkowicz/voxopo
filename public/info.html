How It Works

Three devices, one game. The access code and room code are the two things that move between them.

🔑
Access Code
Given to whoever's hosting tonight.
→ unlocks hosting
📱
Host's Phone
Enters the access code at /host, gets back a room code.
→ room code
📺
TV / Display
That room code gets typed in on the TV, which becomes the shared screen.
→ same code, via QR
👥
Players
Scan the TV's QR code (or type the room code at /play) to join on their own phones.

The one thing to know going in: the room code gets typed in twice on two different devices -- once on the TV, once (per player) on each phone. That's normal, not a bug.

TV Setup

The display just needs any browser pointed at this site. Not every TV has one -- here's what to expect, and what to do if yours doesn't.

TV / SituationHas a browser?Fix
Samsung (Tizen) or LG (webOS) smart TVYesNothing -- open the browser and go.
Google TV / Android TVNoBring your own device (see below)
Apple TVNo, never hasBring your own device (see below)
RokuNo, never hasBring your own device (see below)

Older TV with no smart featuresNoBring your own device (see below)

Bring your own device (works on any TV, no subscription needed)

  • HDMI cable, straight from a laptop -- the simplest option. No WiFi pairing, no account, nothing to buy if you already own the cable. Just plug in and switch the TV's input.
  • Phone + USB-C/Lightning-to-HDMI adapter -- same idea if a laptop isn't handy. Rotate the phone to landscape first and request the "Desktop site" in your browser -- mirroring a phone held upright wastes most of the screen as black bars on a widescreen TV.
  • A personal streaming stick (Chromecast, Fire TV Stick, Roku Stick) -- a one-time purchase, no ongoing subscription required to cast your own browser tab. Needs to join the same WiFi network as the device casting to it, which can be unreliable on hotel guest networks.

How to Play

Trivi-yeah!

Multiple-choice trivia across a mix of subjects. Everyone answers on their own phone; the TV reveals the correct answer and awards points once everyone's locked in (or the clock runs out). Faster correct answers, and a streak of 3-in-a-row, earn bonus recognition on the leaderboard.

Country Monkey

A country is highlighted on the map on the TV -- tap the right one on your phone before time runs out. Covers Africa, the Americas, Asia, Europe, the Middle East, Southeast Asia, and the Pacific Islands.

EmpossDurr

A social-deduction party game. Everyone but one secret impostor gets the same word; the impostor gets only a one-word clue. Talk it out, then vote on who you think is faking it -- or if you're the impostor, declare and try to guess the real word before you're caught.

OutcomePoints

Wrongly accuse someone else−1
Impostor survives a round (no majority reached)+1
Impostor survives via a wrong accusation+3
Impostor declares and guesses right+5
Impostor declares and guesses wrong−2

On The Spectrum

One player sets a "true" answer on a slider for a statement about themselves. Everyone else tries to guess where they landed -- the closer your guess, the more points you earn.

Privacy

We don't store anything about you or your game. Player names, scores, and session info exist only in server memory for as long as your room is active -- nothing is written to a database, and it's gone the moment the room closes or the server restarts. The only things we keep in permanent storage are the trivia/word content itself and a list of access codes -- neither has anything to do with who played or how they did.
