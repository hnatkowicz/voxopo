Voxopo Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 23, 2026

At Voxopo, we believe your phone should be a passport to fun, learning, and human interaction—not a target for digital tracking. Our core cellular architecture is intentionally built from the ground up to respect your personal boundaries.

The Voxopo No-Spy Guarantee

We do not track your device, we do not monitor your habits, and we never collect data to sell to advertisers. When you text our live interactive game boards, your message is parsed strictly to track points for your local room session.

Carrier-Mandated Compliance Clause:
All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with or sold to any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Data Retention

Your room scores and player names exist solely to declare a winner at the end of the night. We run automated database purges to keep our memory clean and your presence private.
