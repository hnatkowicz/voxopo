# Water

Seas, oceans, bays, lakes and rivers, for the "Wet Pants" category in
Thinking Pants (and maybe Country Monkey later).

- **Format:** same recipe as the maps: 1200 x 900 WebP, quality 85,
  lossless off. Keep the SVG master on your computer.
- **Names:** plain, e.g. `caspian-sea.webp`, `lake-victoria.webp`,
  `nile-river.webp`. Players never see filenames.
- **Tips:** draw rivers thick so they read on a TV across the room, and
  ring small lakes the way tiny countries are ringed.
